About

A little about me.

I have a soft spot for messy, data-heavy workflows, and for turning them into screens people barely notice.

I'm Himanshi, a UI/UX Designer at Dancing Numbers in Gurugram. A five-month rotation in backend development and API integration taught me to design with the build in mind. I hold an MCA (CGPA 9.4/10) and the Google UX Design Professional Certificate.

For the past 4+ years, I've designed SaaS and D2C products used by 50,000+ users, from data-heavy dashboards at iRasus Technologies, where a redesign lifted daily active users by 15%, to a QuickBooks automation platform at Dancing Numbers.

Resume

I believe good design shouldn't shout. It should whisper. The best interfaces get out of the way, so people can simply get their work done.
